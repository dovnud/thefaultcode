## Pressure switch codes by brand
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [Goodman EE1](/goodman/ee1/) | Pressure switch circuit closed at start of heating cycle |
| [Goodman EE2](/goodman/ee2/) | Pressure switch circuit not closed |
| [Carrier 23](/carrier/23/) | Pressure switch did not open |
| [Carrier 31](/carrier/31/) | Low or high pressure switch did not close or reopened |
| [Trane E3.1](/trane/e3-1/) / [E3.2](/trane/e3-2/) | 1st stage pressure switch shorted / open |
| [Lennox red off / green slow](/lennox/red-off-green-slow/) | Pressure prove switch open or blocked inlet/exhaust vent |
| [Rheem 44](/rheem/44/) / [45](/rheem/45/) / [46](/rheem/46/) | Low pressure switch closed with inducer off / open with inducer on |

## Causes listed by manufacturers
- Blocked or restricted vent or intake pipe (Rheem FAQ, Lennox, Carrier)
- Inducer (draft) blower not running to speed (Rheem FAQ)
- Disconnected or obstructed pressure tubing (Carrier)
- Water or condensate problems in the collector box (see [Rheem 59](/rheem/59/))
- A damaged heat exchanger (Rheem FAQ)

## Safe checks
- Look at outdoor intake and exhaust terminations for snow, ice, leaves or nests.
- Note the code before power is removed.

Do not jumper or bypass a pressure switch. It protects against venting failures that can put carbon monoxide in the home.

> **Safety first:** If you smell gas, don't touch light switches or use a phone inside. Leave the building and call your gas utility from outside. Turn off power to the furnace before opening any panel, and never bypass or jumper a safety switch. Gas, venting, wiring and control-board work is for a licensed HVAC technician.
