## Lockout timings from the manuals
| Brand / code | What the manual says |
|---|---|
| [Carrier 13](/carrier/13/) / [14](/carrier/14/) | Limit and ignition lockouts auto-reset after 3 hours |
| [Carrier 21](/carrier/21/) | Gas heating lockout: control will NOT auto-reset |
| [Carrier 45](/carrier/45/) | Control circuitry lockout: auto-resets after 1 hour |
| [Trane E2.1](/trane/e2-1/) / [E2.2](/trane/e2-2/) | One-hour lockout after retries or recycles are exceeded |
| [Lennox alternating slow flash](/lennox/alternating-slow-flash/) | Watchguard: 1-hour lockout after repeated ignition failures |
| [Rheem 10](/rheem/10/) | One-hour lockout after repeated faults |

## A safe reset
1. Note the code on the control board.
2. Set the thermostat to off, wait, then back to heat. Or switch the furnace's service switch off for a few seconds and back on.
3. Watch for the furnace to start normally. If the same code returns, stop resetting.

## When not to reset
- **Rollout switch tripped:** Carrier's manual says the flame rollout switch requires a manual reset and Rheem warns not to reset safety switches without correcting the cause. Rollout means flames came out of the heat exchanger area; this needs a technician.
- **You smell gas:** leave and call your gas utility.
- **Repeated lockouts:** the cause is still present.

> **Safety first:** If you smell gas, don't touch light switches or use a phone inside. Leave the building and call your gas utility from outside. Turn off power to the furnace before opening any panel, and never bypass or jumper a safety switch. Gas, venting, wiring and control-board work is for a licensed HVAC technician.
